COGNEXA, the Cognitive Nexus for Assessment

Beyond assessment. Towards learning.

An online assessment portal for institutes. Students write their answers on screen, AI marks each written answer against the faculty's own rubric, and the faculty approves before those marks reach the student.

Philosophy

  1. AI recommends.

    The AI reads each answer against the rubric and model answer the faculty wrote. It proposes marks for every rubric point, quotes the student's words it relied on, and lists what was missing. It is a draft, and it is shown as one.

  2. Faculty decides.

    The faculty sees the draft beside the answer, changes any mark, adds a remark, and approves. For mid-term and sessional tests nothing reaches a student until the faculty has approved it.

  3. Student learns.

    The student gets more than a number: marks for each rubric point, the points they missed, and what to improve. The result stays in their login to come back to.

Vision

Every assessment should leave the student knowing what they got right, what they missed, and what to do next.

Mission

  • Give every student feedback on every answer, not only a total.
  • Keep the teacher in charge of every mark that counts.
  • Take the repetitive first reading of answers off the faculty's desk.
  • Give institutes clean, printable assessment records for outcome-based education.

How it works

  1. The institute sets up its profile

    Name, logo, courses, sections, subjects, and the faculty and student lists. Staff get only the access the institute gives them.

  2. The faculty builds an assessment

    Choose the kind of assessment, then set the questions, marks, course outcomes, model answers and rubric.

  3. Students write it online

    A timed window with autosave. Pasting is blocked and leaving the page is recorded for the faculty.

  4. AI drafts, the faculty approves

    Objective questions are checked against the key. Written answers get an AI draft that the faculty reviews and approves.

  5. Results and records

    Students see their feedback. The faculty gets class, question-wise and CO-wise analysis, and print-ready reports.

What an institute gets

Marking against your rubric
The AI awards marks only against the criteria the faculty set, never on a scheme of its own.
Approval before release
The faculty can moderate every mark. Marks they change are recorded as moderated.
Access by role
Institute admin, nodal officers, heads of department, faculty and students each see only what they are given.
Course outcome analysis
Question-wise and CO-wise performance for each assessment, against a target the institute sets.
Records for the file
Assessment report, question paper, answer key and evaluated answer sheets, with your institute's name and logo.
An integrity log
Paste attempts and page switches are counted for each student and shown to the faculty.
